4. Children's Personal Data Safeguards (DPDP Section 9)

Under Section 2(f) and Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, a child is defined as an individual under 18 years of age.

  • No Tracking or Profiling: TaskFarmm strictly prohibits tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • Age Affirmation: Registration requires explicit confirmation that the user is at least 18 years of age.
  • Inadvertent Submission: If we discover that personal data of an individual under 18 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, such data will be immediately erased.

5. Data Principal Rights & Self-Service Workflows

Under the DPDP Act 2023, every Data Principal possesses statutory rights that can be exercised directly through our interactive Privacy & Data Rights Center:

Right to Access (Section 11)

Access a structured summary of all personal data processed, sharing identities, and download your complete Personal Data Dossier (JSON/HTML).

Right to Correction (Section 12)

Correct inaccurate personal data, complete incomplete information, and update your profile details with immediate downstream synchronization.

Right to Erasure (Section 12(3))

Request permanent deletion of your account, files, projects, tasks, comments, and notifications when processing is no longer required.

Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4))

Withdraw optional consents (notifications, AI processing, updates) at any time with ease comparable to giving consent.

Right to Nominate (Section 14)

Designate an authorized representative/nominee who can exercise your data principal rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

Submit privacy grievances to our Grievance Redressal Officer with guaranteed statutory resolution within 90 days.
